      Connect the Facebook page
      1. On developers.facebook.com/apps, make an app (type: Business) and add Facebook Login for Business.
      2. In its Facebook Login settings, add https://app.jslandscapingmd.com/api/meta/callback as a valid redirect address.
      3. Paste its App ID and App secret here and press Save.
      4. Press Connect and choose the JS Landscaping page.
