## What's new
- Updates now ask first. When a new Helper Buddies version is out, this screen pops up in game with what's new and a Yes / Not now choice.
- Pressing Yes downloads it in the background while you keep playing. It goes live the next time you open Minecraft.
- The mod checks for updates every 30 minutes while you play, not only at launch.
- Release notes like these, with screenshots, show right in the update screen.
- New command: /helperupdate brings the update screen back.

## What to look for
- After this version, the next update should pop up a few seconds after you join a world.
- Try "Not now": a green [Show update] link appears in chat. Click it to bring the screen back.
- Press "Yes, update", close Minecraft, open it again, and check the version in the Mods menu.
