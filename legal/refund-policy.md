# Refund Policy and [[GUARANTEE NAME]]

**DRAFT — REQUIRES ATTORNEY REVIEW**

_Version: 0.1-draft · Effective date: TBD_

## 1. The [[GUARANTEE NAME]]

If our first application of the season at your property does not meet your
expectations, tell us within seven (7) days of that first application and we
will refund your Winter Pass in full. This is a one-time, first-visit guarantee
and applies once per property per season.

## 2. Cancellation before the season starts

You may cancel for a full refund at any time before the season start date shown
at checkout, or before our first application at your property, whichever comes
first.

## 3. Cancellation during the season

After the first application, and after the guarantee window in Section 1 has
passed, the Pass is non-refundable, except:

- **Move or sale of the property.** We will refund the unused portion of the
  season on a pro-rated basis, or transfer the Pass to the new owner at no
  charge.
- **Our failure to perform.** If we fail to treat your property for a
  qualifying event and do not remedy it within 24 hours of your notice, you may
  request a pro-rated refund for the remainder of the season.

## 4. Monthly plans

If you are on a monthly plan and cancel during the season under Section 3,
remaining installments are cancelled and any pro-rated amount owed to you is
refunded to the original payment method.

## 5. Low-snow seasons

The Winter Pass is priced as a season membership, not per application.
Because it includes forecast monitoring and guaranteed readiness, a season with
few or no qualifying events does not by itself entitle you to a refund.

## 6. How to request a refund

Text or email us at [[PHONE]] or [[EMAIL]] with your name and service
address. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within ten (10)
business days.
