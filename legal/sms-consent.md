## Transactional messages (part of the Winter Pass)

By purchasing a Winter Pass and providing your mobile number, you agree to
receive service text messages from [[COMPANY NAME]] about your account. These
include forecast watch notices, "on our way" and "job complete" messages with
photos, billing notices, and end-of-season summaries. Message frequency varies
with the weather. Message and data rates may apply.

## Marketing messages (separate, optional consent)

By checking the marketing consent box or replying to our price-sheet text
offer, you agree to receive recurring marketing and promotional text messages
from [[COMPANY NAME]] at the number you provided, including pricing, seasonal
offers, and renewal reminders. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

## Opting out

Reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving messages of that type. Reply
**HELP** for help. You can also contact us at [[PHONE]] or [[EMAIL]].

## Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
