[[COMPANY NAME]] ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we
collect when you visit our website or purchase a Winter Pass, and how we use it.

## 1. What we collect

- **Order information.** Name, service address, email, phone number, plan and
  add-on selections, and payment status. Payment card details are collected and
  stored by our payment processor (Stripe); we never see your full card number.
- **Site usage.** Pages viewed, buttons clicked, device type, referring site,
  and advertising campaign identifiers (UTM parameters). We collect this with
  our own first-party analytics.
- **Legal acceptance records.** When you accept our terms at checkout we record
  the version of each document you agreed to, the time, and your IP address.
- **Property information.** The approximate size of your driveway and walkways,
  which we use to price and plan service.

## 2. How we use it

To provide and schedule the Services, to send transactional messages, to
improve our website and offers, to measure advertising, and to comply with law.

## 3. Who we share it with

- **Service providers** who help us run the business: our payment processor
  (Stripe), our customer relationship system (GoHighLevel), our hosting
  providers (Vercel, Supabase), and our text and email delivery providers.
- **Advertising platforms.** We may share hashed contact information with
  Google Ads to measure which advertisements led to purchases (enhanced
  conversions). We do not sell your personal information.
- **Legal.** When required by law or to protect our rights.

## 4. Your choices

You may opt out of marketing text messages at any time by replying STOP. You
may request a copy of, or deletion of, your personal information by contacting
us at [[EMAIL]]. We will retain order and legal acceptance records as required
for tax, accounting, and dispute purposes.

## 5. Cookies

We use a small number of first-party cookies to remember your session and
which version of our site you were shown. We do not use third-party tracking
cookies other than those required for advertising measurement described above.

## 6. Children

Our Services are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.
