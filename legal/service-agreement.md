# Winter Pass Service Agreement

**DRAFT — REQUIRES ATTORNEY REVIEW**

_Version: 0.1-draft · Effective date: TBD_

This Service Agreement describes exactly what [[COMPANY NAME]] will do under
each Winter Pass plan, and what triggers a visit.

## 1. Season

The service season runs from the season start date through the season end date
shown at checkout (target: November 15 through March 31). Applications outside
the season are not included.

## 2. Salt Pre-Book (all plans)

We will apply a calcium chloride based de-icer to the driveway, walkways, and
front steps of the property listed on your order.

**Dispatch trigger.** We monitor the forecast for your property. An
application is scheduled automatically whenever the forecast for Harford County
calls for freezing rain, sleet, ice, or [[SNOW_INCHES_TRIGGER]] or more inches
of snow. Depending on the event, we may treat before the event (anti-icing),
after the event (de-icing), or both.

**No action required.** You do not need to call, text, or schedule. We will
text you when a crew is on the way and again when the application is complete,
with a photo where practical.

**Number of applications.** The plan covers all qualifying events during the
season. There is no per-storm charge and no cap on qualifying visits.

**Areas covered.** The treated area is limited to the paved driveway, paved
walkways, and front entry steps as measured at checkout. Rear patios, decks,
and non-paved surfaces are not included unless added to your order.

## 3. Pet-Friendly De-Icer (add-on or included, per plan)

If your plan includes the Pet-Friendly De-Icer, every application at your
property will use a pet-safe de-icing formulation in place of the standard
product. We will not knowingly apply a non-pet-safe product to a property with
this option.

## 4. Winter Schedule Hold (per plan)

The Winter Schedule Hold reserves a place on our plow and clearing route for the
entire season. Route capacity is limited and holds are sold on a first-come
basis. A hold guarantees that your property is on the route when a qualifying
snow event occurs; per-event clearing terms and any per-event fees are stated at
checkout.

## 5. Timing

We aim to complete applications before the morning commute for overnight events
and within a reasonable window after daytime events. Timing depends on storm
severity, road conditions, and safety. We do not guarantee specific arrival
times.

## 6. Property access and conditions

You agree to provide reasonable access. We may skip an area that is blocked by
vehicles or other obstructions and will note this in the completion message.
Repeated inaccessibility does not entitle you to a refund for the missed area.

## 7. Materials

Our standard product is a calcium chloride based de-icer selected because it
performs at lower temperatures than rock salt and is gentler on concrete,
pavers, and lawn edges when applied at label rates. No de-icer is completely
without effect on all surfaces; we apply at manufacturer-recommended rates.

## 8. Weather that does not occur

The Winter Pass is a readiness membership. If the season produces few or no
qualifying events, the Pass has still provided monitoring and guaranteed
readiness. Refunds for low-snow seasons are governed by the Refund Policy.

## 9. Change of ownership

If you sell the property during the season, you may transfer the remaining term
of the Pass to the new owner at no charge by notifying us, or cancel per the
Refund Policy.
