## 3. Migrations

`supabase/migrations/0364_back_office.sql` is additive only. Every new table has RLS on.

- **`back_office_settings`**: one row per org. Org-scoped policy. It holds:
  - the switches and their `*_started_at` stamps;
  - the default rates (account manager 7, evaluator 4, affiliate 4);
  - the hold-back settings, reminder days and the fertilizer guard switch.
- **`commission_rates`**: a person's rate on file for each share (account_manager, evaluator, affiliate). Policy: org + `is_admin()`. Kept separate from `profiles.commission_pct`, which stays the account manager's pre-pool deal.
- **`commission_accruals`**: one row per payment × person × share × portion (collection or holdback). Unique on `(org, source_key, profile, role, portion)`, so writing it twice is harmless. Policy: org + admin.
- **`commission_payout_batches`**: one per week, unique on `(org, week_start)`. Status is awaiting_approval, approved or void. Policy: org + admin.
- **`commission_statements`**: one per person per batch. The lines are jsonb and record any `offset` absorbed by hand payments. Policy: org + admin.
- **`compliance_obligations`**: the calendar. Org-scoped policy. Each row has:
  - a due date and recurrence;
  - `date_confirmed`, which seeds as false and shows as "confirm the date";
  - an active flag, credits, insurance details and a document path;
  - a `seed_key`, unique per org.
- **`client_cois`**: generated COIs, with the stored PDF path and sent_at / sent_to. Org-scoped policy.
- **`onboarding_checklists`** (unique applicant_id, unique profile_id) and **`onboarding_items`** (unique checklist_id + key). Policy: org + admin.
- **Storage buckets `compliance-docs` and `hr-docs`**: private, with no policies. All access goes through signed URLs that the server mints after an owner-level check.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| `commission_accrual_on` | **off**; the first switch-on stamps `commission_accrual_started_at` |
| `default_account_manager_pct` / `default_evaluator_pct` / `default_affiliate_pct` | 7 / 4 / 4 |
| `commission_holdback_on` | **off** |
| `holdback_collect_pct` | 50; the rest waits until the job is completed at the target margin or better |
| `holdback_margin_pct` | 40 |
| `holdback_crew_cost_per_hour` | $35; used to work out margin from `final_crew_hours` |
| `compliance_reminders_on` | **off**; stamps started_at, and seeds the Maryland defaults if the calendar is empty |
| `reminder_days` | {60, 30, 7} |
| `onboarding_on` | **off**; stamps started_at |
| `fertilizer_guard_on` | **on**; a check that sends nothing and affects only new bookings and approvals |

**Backlog rule.** Each automation only acts after its switch-on:
- Accrual only counts money paid or received on or after started_at.
- Onboarding only covers people hired or created after started_at.
- Compliance reminders only cover obligations created or edited after started_at. Seeded defaults stay quiet until the owner confirms or edits the date.

### Design decisions

- **Shares.** Accrual uses the same pool split as the Money page (`sharesFor` / `usesSplit`).
- **Rates.** A share uses the person's `commission_rates` row if they have one, otherwise the default from settings. Jobs sold before the split keep the account manager's `commission_pct` (default 15).
- **When a share is payable without hold-back.** Same rule as the existing `commission.ts`:
  - an affiliate's share on collection;
  - an account manager's or evaluator's share at sign-off (job completed, no open tickets);
  - an evaluator's share held while there are site-map issues.
- **Hold-back.** Applies to account managers and evaluators only, never to affiliates.
- **Hand payments.** Hand payments recorded after the switch are subtracted from statement lines, and each line tracks how much it absorbed. A week fully covered by hand payments settles itself at $0 without asking for approval.
- **Exceptions.** `job_exceptions` requires a `job_id`, so compliance and HR exceptions can't use it. They are surfaced through the needs-you strips, the queue feed, and one alert to the owner.

### Not finished, and known risks

- **No void or un-approve UI** for a weekly batch. The Money page's existing `undoCommissionPaid` can delete individual payout rows.
- **The COI is not an insurer-issued ACORD 25.** It is a summary of the policies on file, with a disclaimer, and it tells the client to ask the agent for the ACORD form or an endorsement.
- **The COI email sends a link, not an attachment.** It is a 30-day signed link, because `sendClientMessage` doesn't support attachments.
- **The COI card shows on every client page.** Customers have no "commercial" flag, so it can't be limited to commercial clients.
- **Hold-back margin is mostly unknown for now.** Margin is computed from the final crew hours, materials and other costs against the agreed price, and most jobs don't have final costs recorded yet (hotspot 6). Held-back amounts will show "margin unknown" until job costing is filled in.
- **Gaps in the blackout guard.** It does not yet check:
  - the schedule engine's own insert (`schedule-engine-actions.ts`);
  - the day a client picks on the public proposal page.
- **How the blackout is read.**
  - "Until Dec 1" is treated as through Nov 30, the conservative reading.
  - Fertilizer is detected from wording. Lime, potash and weed control are allowed.
- **The seeded dates are Maryland defaults** that the owner must confirm:
  - MDA renewals on June 30 and the fertilizer certification on Dec 31;
  - quarterly 941 filings, W-2s by Jan 31, SDAT by Apr 15 and income tax by Mar 15.

  MHIC and H-2B start inactive. Insurance and vehicle dates start blank.
- **The first day defaults to the hire date.** A new hire's first day is taken from the hire decision or account creation, and can be edited in the panel. The I-9's 3-business-day deadline ignores holidays.
- **Role fixes have limits.** A fix requires the role to already exist in `roles`, and won't remove the caller's own owner role.
